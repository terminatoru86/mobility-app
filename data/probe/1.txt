<h2 id="what-can-you-build" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#what-can-you-build">What Can You Build?</a></h2>
<p><a href="https://bikeep.com">Bikeep</a> provides smart bike parking and IoT access control hardware for cities, campuses, and transit hubs. The API gives you full control:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Bike parking API</strong> — Reserve, lock, and unlock smart lockers and bike docks from your application</li>
<li><strong>Bike room access control</strong> — Manage RFID allowlists for enclosed bike rooms, hangars, and secure storage</li>
<li><strong>Availability displays</strong> — Show real-time bike parking availability on campus maps, city dashboards, or mobile apps</li>
<li><strong>IoT monitoring</strong> — Track device health, connectivity, and alarms across your fleet</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="getting-credentials" tabindex="-1"><a class="header-anchor" href="#getting-credentials">Getting Credentials</a></h2>
<p>To start using the Bikeep API, you need:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>API credentials</strong> — A <code>client_id</code> and <code>client_secret</code> for OAuth2 authentication</li>
<li><strong>Operator ID</strong> — Your organization’s UUID in the Bikeep system</li>
</ol>
<p>Request credentials by emailing <a href="mailto:info@bikeep.com?subject=Bikeep%20API%20credentials">info@bikeep.com</a>.</p>
